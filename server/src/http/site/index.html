How it works

Reading labels is hard. Tiny print, strange names, forty ingredients. Tappy reads them for you.

01

Tell Tappy what you avoid.

Pick from 50+ diets, allergies and conditions, from gluten free and low FODMAP to pregnancy. Set how strict each one is.

02

Point. Scan. Done.

Aim your camera at any barcode. Tappy locks on in a beat, with a little buzz so you know it got it.

03

Get a verdict you can read.

Avoid, limit, or no conflicts found. Every ingredient that matters to you is flagged and explained, including "may contain".

04

Find what works. Keep it.

Browse the grocery aisles filtered to your profile and save the winners into lists like Weekly Shopping or Want to Try.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is Tappy medical advice?

No. Tappy is a guide based on the information on package labels, which can be incomplete or out of date. If you have a severe allergy, always read the package yourself.

Where does the product information come from?

From Open Food Facts (open database licence ODbL) and the U.S. Department of Agriculture FoodData Central, plus label photos shared by Tappy users.

What if a product isn't found?

Snap a photo of the ingredient list and Tappy reads it for you. If there's no ingredient list at all, Tappy says so instead of guessing.

Which diets and allergies does it cover?

More than 50: the major allergens, plus diets and conditions like gluten free, vegan, low FODMAP, low histamine, seed oil free, pregnancy, GERD, PCOS and many more. You can also add individual ingredients to avoid.
