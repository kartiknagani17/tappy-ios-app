# Reel analysis: 8 reference reels → Tappy plan

All 8 are 720×1280 (9:16), 30 fps, and 19–56 s long. 7 of them advertise **Ivy Food scanner** (@ivyfoodapp) and 1 advertises **Mise** (a meal planner).
Analysed with ffmpeg (scene cuts, contact sheets) and faster-whisper (speech) from D:\AIVideoEditor2\Setup only.

## The formula (shared by 7 of 8)

| Beat | Time | What's on screen | Who/what is needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | 0–4 s | Face to camera in a store aisle or kitchen, holding a product. Big bold caption: white box, black condensed font (Anton/Bebas style), 2–3 lines | **Face** |
| 2. Product shot | ~2 s | Close-up of the product in hand on the shelf, with a white camera-viewfinder bracket overlay ("scanning") | Hands only |
| 3. App proof | 5–15 s per product | Full-screen **screen recording** of the app's result: score (52/100, 0/100, 91/100), additives, risks, scrolling. Watermark "@handle" in a black box | App screen recording |
| 4. Repeat 2–3 | ×3 products | "First up… Next up… Lastly…" listicle | Hands + app |
| 5. CTA | last 3–5 s | Face again: "Comment IVY / APP and I'll send you the link" + "see below for swaps" | **Face** |

The face is on screen for only ~15–25 % of the runtime. Everything else is hands, products and app screens.

Captions: speech-synced, small, black text on a white box, near the bottom-centre. Cuts come every 2–6 s.
The voiceover is a calm first-person creator voice. Two reels (70215, 70720) have no voice: just music and on-screen text.

## The hooks that work (adapt; don't copy)
- Fear/warning: "This food ___ ".
- Identity + "I stopped buying": "Products I stopped buying at ___ after ___".
- Listicle: "3 ___ I'll never ___ again".
- Positive reversal: "3 'unhealthy' foods I say YES to".
- Parent guilt: "___ are the new junk food and most parents have no idea".
- Discovery: "Been ___ for 13 years and NOW I find this".

## Tappy versions (diet-fit, not a health score)
1. "Things I stopped buying at Trader Joe's after going dairy-free" → scan 3 → Tappy shows the hidden milk (whey, casein, Milchpulver).
2. "3 'vegan' snacks that AREN'T vegan" → scan → verdict.
3. "Being gluten-free for 5 years and NOW I find this 🤯" → Mise-style onboarding walkthrough, phone only.
4. "Stop reading labels in the aisle" → one scan, instant verdict.
5. "Kids' snacks most allergy parents get wrong".

## ⚠️ Don't copy these parts
- **"After my cancer diagnosis" / "8yr cancer survivor" claims from an AI person.** This is a fabricated personal testimonial. It breaks FTC endorsement rules and TikTok/Meta policy, and it's the fastest way to get banned. Give the AI character a persona that doesn't claim a real medical history (for example "the label-reading friend"), and turn on the platform's "AI-generated" label.
- **"Increases cancer risk"-style claims.** Stick to what Tappy actually does: "contains milk", "not vegan", "fits your diet".
- Ivy's exact scripts, @handle, or UI look. Use the same format with your own words.
