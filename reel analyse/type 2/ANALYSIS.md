## Structure (R1 / R2 / R4, the main template)

| Section | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook (character) | 0.00-3.00 s (90 frames) | One continuous shot, fixed camera at chest height in a kitchen/living room. She starts with her face right at the lens (cut off by the frame), then steps back over ~1.3 s to a full-body standing pose and stares at the camera with a serious, neutral face. Hands at her sides, no talking, no gestures. **R1, R2 and R4 use the exact same hook clip**; only the text changes. |
| 2. Poster montage | 3.00 s to the end (or to the CTA) | Movie/doc posters, **one per beat**. Each is held exactly **0.667 s (20 frames)**, with hard cuts, no transitions, no zoom/pan (static: SSIM 0.999 between the first and last frame of a poster), and no text on top. ## Timing and music
- The same track in R1, R2 and R4, starting from the same point. It's a sparse percussive beat (knocks/claps), about **90 BPM**, with a hit every ~0.33 s and a strong hit on every beat (0.667 s).
- The cuts are exactly on the beat: 3.00, 3.67, 4.33, 5.00, 5.67, 6.33, 7.00, 7.67, 8.33 ... The hook ends on a strong hit at 2.99 s. So the hook = 4.5 beats and each poster = 1 beat.
- No voiceover and no dialogue. The music carries it. Loudness: mean -17.9 dB, peaks at 0 dB.

## Text overlay
- Font: a white serif (Instagram's "Classic"-style), centred, with a soft dark shadow and no box. It sits in the upper third, roughly 18-25% from the top, and is ~2 lines plus a tag line.
- Hook text: "Documentaries to watch / if you're waking up", then a blank line, then "Part 3" / "Part 2" / "Fertility Edition".
  - R1: everything is shown from frame 0.
  - R2: "Part 2" pops in at ~0.77 s (the title first, then the part number).
- The text disappears at the first poster. The posters carry their own titles.
- CTA text: two short sentences in the same font, slightly smaller, near the top (~12-25%).

## How it's made (recipe)
1. Film one 3 s hook of the character (a step back from the lens to a standing stare). It's reusable for every part.
2. Pick 8-10 posters and fit each one to 1080x1920.
3. Cut on a ~90 BPM beat: hook to beat 4.5 (3.0 s), then 1 poster per beat (0.667 s).
4. Add the hook text in a white serif, upper third: the title + "Part N" or "<Topic> Edition".
5. Optional CTA ending (~3.8 s): the character again + 2 lines of text + an app screenshot popped in bottom-right ~1 s later.
6. The caption drives the funnel: "Comment 'app' and I'll send you ...".
7. Total: 9-12 s, so it loops quickly. The Part-N series format makes people follow for the next part.
